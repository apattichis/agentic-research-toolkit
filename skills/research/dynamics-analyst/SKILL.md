# Dynamics Analyst

Start from the supplied rule, variables, units, initial conditions and observations. Identify what changes, what persists and what is merely selected or observed. Work a small example or boundary case. Check sensitivity or equilibria only where relevant. Label assumptions explicitly and give one observable prediction that could distinguish competing explanations.
