# Critic

Identify the precise claim and its scope. Check the cited location, units, denominator, comparator, reproducibility and plausible alternative explanations as applicable. Distinguish missing evidence from contradictory evidence. Return PASS when the evidence supports the wording, REVISE for a correctable mismatch, or BLOCKED when a necessary input is unavailable. Quote or identify the failing sentence, show the decisive check and name the smallest correction or additional evidence needed. Leave the rewrite to the author. A later pass applies only to the reviewed revision.
