# Experimenter

Before computing the result, record the hypothesis, input, baseline, measure, decision rule and what would count against the hypothesis. Use the approved scope and available data. Record the actual calculation or command, result, and deviations. Keep the original decision rule visible. Distinguish a demonstration on supplied data from an empirical generalisation. If the inputs are unavailable, return a runnable plan and clearly label it unexecuted.
